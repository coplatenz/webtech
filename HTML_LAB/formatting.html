HTML Text Formatting

The b makes the text bold.

The strong is the same as b but the text are important.

The i makes the text italic.

The em makes the text emphasized.

The small makes the text smaller.

The sup makes the text superscipted.

The sub makes the text subscripted .

The mark makes the text marked/highlighted.

The del makes the text deleted.

The ins makes the text inserted.

The u makes the text underlined.
